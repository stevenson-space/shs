App support

Contact the developer

For iOS app help, feedback, or feature requests, email this monitored inbox:

admin@stevenson.space

Include your app version (in Settings → About), iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it. For a schedule or menu issue, include the date and period or menu station.

Please do not send your student ID screenshot, student number, barcode, password, or other sensitive student information. Redact these details from any screenshots you share.

Import a student ID

  1. Open Infinite Campus and take a screenshot of your own Student Profile page, including your student details and the entire barcode near the bottom.
  2. Open Stevenson Space, go to ID, and tap Choose Screenshot.
  3. Select the screenshot in Apple’s photo picker. Review the extracted card, then tap Save.
  4. To scan your saved ID, tap the card or Show for Scanning.

Only import your own ID. The screenshot is processed on your device; you do not enter your Infinite Campus credentials in Stevenson Space. ID import is optional, and schedules and menus work without it.

The screenshot could not be read

Take a fresh, clear screenshot of the whole Student Profile page. Keep the barcode and printed student number fully visible, and avoid cropping or editing the image. Tap Try Another Screenshot or Choose a Different Screenshot to retry. If the image is in iCloud Photos, connect to the internet so Apple can download it.

If the barcode and printed number do not match, capture a fresh screenshot from your current profile. The app reads ID details from the image; they cannot be typed in manually.

My name or photo is missing, or my ID is out of date

Capture the whole Student Profile page so the name and photo are included. A missing photo produces a silhouette. To import a new image, open ID → ID options (the three-dot menu) and choose Replace Screenshot, then review and save the new card.

The barcode will not scan

Tap Show for Scanning to display the barcode full screen and hold it steady for the scanner. If your school ID has changed, use Replace Screenshot to import a fresh image. If it still fails, email the developer with the scanner location and what happened, without sending your barcode or student number.

Manage notifications

  1. In the app’s Settings, turn on End-of-period heads-up and/or Morning alert on special days.
  2. Allow notifications when iOS asks. If you previously declined, open iPhone Settings → Notifications → SHS Space and enable Allow Notifications, then reopen the app and turn on the reminders you want.
  3. Choose your end-of-period lead time (0–15 minutes) and the time for the morning alert. A lead time of zero alerts at the bell.

Morning alerts are for special schedule days. Reminders use the school’s Central Time schedule and are scheduled locally on your device.

A reminder did not arrive

Check both the app’s reminder toggles and iOS notification permission. Focus modes, notification delivery settings, or Scheduled Summary may silence or delay an alert. Reopen the app periodically to refresh upcoming reminders, especially after changing your schedule or notification settings.

For iOS delivery controls, see Apple’s notification settings guide. To stop reminders, turn them off in the app or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings.

Update schedules & lunch menus

The app checks for public schedule-date and lunch-menu updates when it opens or returns to the foreground, normally at most once an hour. Connect to the internet and reopen the app to let it check. Previously saved content stays available when a download fails.

After five days without a successful update check, the relevant Home or Lunch screen shows a reminder with Check for Updates. Tap it to retry immediately. This button appears with the reminder; it is not a permanent Settings control.

Bell times and supported school-year boundaries ship with the app. Keep the app updated through the App Store (or TestFlight for a beta) to receive changes to those.

The school day or my class details look wrong

Check which date is selected on Home. In Settings → My Schedule, review your class names, rooms, lunch, and advisory assignments. A manual day override takes priority over downloaded schedule dates; review Schedule Override in Settings and delete an override if it is no longer needed.

To set a day manually, use Settings → Set a Day’s Schedule. If the published schedule still appears incorrect, email the date, expected schedule, and app version. Follow the school’s announcements for last-minute changes.

The lunch menu is missing or seems outdated

Check the selected date in Lunch, then reconnect and reopen the app. Use Check for Updates if the reminder is visible. Menu downloads keep the last successful copy if any station fails to update. A date outside the app’s supported menu range may have no menu; check for an app update and contact the developer if needed.

Remove an ID & manage privacy

Open ID → ID options → Remove ID to remove saved ID details and the cropped photo from the app. If removal fails, unlock your device and try again. Hide ID Photo only changes visibility; it does not delete the photo.

Remove ID does not delete your original screenshot in Photos or copies in existing device backups. Remove your ID before deleting the app; deleting or offloading the app should not be used as a substitute for Remove ID.

Read the iOS app privacy policy for details about local processing, backups, public downloads, and email correspondence. Privacy and deletion requests for information you have emailed to the developer can go to privacy@stevenson.space.
